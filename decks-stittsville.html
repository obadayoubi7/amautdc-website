Composite Decking

The top choice for Stittsville homeowners. Composite boards are colour-stable, slip-resistant, and splinter-free. They won't fade, crack, or absorb moisture through Ottawa's harsh winters. Brands like Trex and TimberTech available.

Pressure-Treated Wood

The most affordable deck option. Properly installed PT lumber delivers a strong, traditional deck at the best price point. Ideal for budget-conscious Stittsville homeowners who don't want to sacrifice structural quality.

Many Stittsville homes have walk-out basements and sloped yards — perfect for multi-level decks. We design connected platforms with integrated stairs, landings, and railings to maximize your outdoor living space.

Custom design  |  Engineered for your yard

100% synthetic, completely waterproof decking with a lifetime manufacturer's warranty. Ideal for Stittsville homes with pools or covered decks. Stays cool underfoot, resists staining, and cleans up with soap and water.
