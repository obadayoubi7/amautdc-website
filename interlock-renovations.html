Interlock & Hardscaping

Driveways, Patios &
Walkways

Interlock transforms your driveway, patio, or walkway into a stunning, durable hardscape. We use premium paving stones that lock together for long-lasting stability through Ottawa's freeze-thaw cycles — no cracking, no crumbling, and easy to repair if ever needed.

Interlock Applications

What We Install

Interlock Driveways

Replace cracked asphalt or dull concrete with a stunning interlock driveway. Paving stones handle Ottawa's freeze-thaw far better than monolithic surfaces — individual stones flex with ground movement rather than cracking. Available in dozens of colours and laying patterns.

Most popular application  |  Major curb appeal upgrade

Patios & Outdoor Living

An interlock patio creates a durable, beautiful foundation for your outdoor living space. We design custom patterns, incorporate built-in borders, and work around existing landscaping features. Pairs beautifully with our deck builds and pergola installations.

Custom patterns  |  Pairs with deck builds

Walkways & Paths

A well-designed front walkway is the first thing guests notice. We install interlock front entry paths, side yard walkways, and garden paths — from simple straight runs to sweeping curved designs with decorative borders and accent stones.

Any shape or pattern  |  Decorative options available

Steps & Retaining Walls

Interlock steps and retaining walls solve grade changes beautifully while adding structure to your yard. We build block retaining walls to manage slopes, prevent erosion, and create tiered garden areas — all using materials that complement your interlock.

Engineered for Ottawa grades  |  Block wall specialists

Pool Surrounds

The area around your pool demands slip-resistant, heat-tolerant, and water-resistant surfacing. Interlock paving stones check every box — they're slip-resistant when wet, stay cool underfoot, and drain naturally between joints. Available in tumbled and smooth finishes.

Slip-resistant finishes  |  Pool-spec installation

Commercial Interlock

Parking areas, plaza entrances, and commercial courtyards benefit from interlock's durability and professional appearance. We install commercial-grade paving systems rated for vehicle loads, with proper drainage engineering included in every project.

Vehicle load rated  |  Drainage engineering included

Why Interlock

Better Than Concrete
or Asphalt

❄️

Ottawa Winter-Proof

Individual paving stones flex independently through freeze-thaw cycles. Unlike concrete or asphalt, interlock doesn't develop stress cracks from heaving. If a stone ever shifts, it can be reset individually — no saw cutting, no patching, no mess.

🎨

Stunning Curb Appeal

Dozens of stone colours, textures, and laying patterns give you complete control over the look. From classic herringbone to contemporary linear, we design installations that complement your home's architecture and landscaping.

🔧

Easy to Repair

One of interlock's best-kept advantages: if a utility ever needs to dig up your driveway, the stones are simply lifted, the work is done, and the stones are relaid — exactly as before. No patching, no colour mismatch, no permanent scar.
