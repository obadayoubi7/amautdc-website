## 3. Review Response Templates

Respond to every review — Google factors response rate/speed into local ranking, and it shows prospective customers you're engaged.

**5-star response:**
> Thank you so much, [Name]! We're thrilled you're happy with your new [fence/deck/interlock] in [City]. Our crew works hard to get every detail right, and it means a lot to hear that come through in the finished project. Thanks for choosing AMA United!

1. **Q: Do you serve all of Ottawa or specific areas?**
   A: We serve all of Ottawa including Kanata, Barrhaven, Orléans, Nepean, Stittsville, Gloucester, Manotick, Riverside South, Findlay Creek, and Greely. Travel is always included in your quote.

2. **Q: Do I need a permit for a fence or deck?**
   A: Most fences under 2 metres (6.5 ft) don't require a permit. Decks over 24 inches above grade or attached to the house do require a City of Ottawa building permit — we handle the full application process for you.

3. **Q: How long does a typical fence installation take?**
   A: Most residential fence installs take 1–2 days. We dig and set posts on day one, then return once the concrete cures to finish rails and boards.

4. **Q: Do you offer free quotes?**
   A: Yes — we visit your property, assess the job, and provide a detailed written quote at no cost, with no obligation.

5. **Q: What warranty do you offer?**
   A: Every job comes with a 2-year workmanship warranty. PVC fencing also carries a lifetime material warranty from the manufacturer.
