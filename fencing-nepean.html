In most cases, fences under 2 metres (6.5 ft) in Nepean do not require a permit from the City of Ottawa. Corner lots, properties near easements, and fences adjacent to city pathways may have restrictions. AMA United reviews City of Ottawa bylaws for your specific Nepean property before every project and handles permit applications if required.

PVC and hybrid fencing perform best in Nepean's climate. Both are engineered to handle extreme freeze-thaw cycles without cracking or warping, and PVC comes with a lifetime material warranty. Cedar is also a strong option when properly treated and sealed before winter.
