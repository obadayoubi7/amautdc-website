Kanata Fencing FAQ

Common Questions

In most cases, fences under 2 metres (6.5 ft) in Kanata do not require a permit from the City of Ottawa. Corner lots, properties near easements, and fences adjacent to city pathways or parks may have restrictions. AMA United reviews City of Ottawa bylaws for your specific Kanata property before every project and handles permit applications if required.

PVC and hybrid fencing perform best in Kanata's west-end climate. Both are engineered to handle extreme freeze-thaw cycles without cracking or warping, and PVC comes with a lifetime material warranty. Cedar is also a strong option when properly treated and sealed before winter.

Most residential fence installs in Kanata take 1–2 days depending on length and style. We dig and set posts in concrete on day one and return to attach rails and boards once the concrete has cured — a finished fence within 48 hours of our start date.
